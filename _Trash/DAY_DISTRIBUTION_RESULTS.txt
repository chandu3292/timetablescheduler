🔧 HOW IT WORKS:

STEP 1: Day-Filling with Instructor Priorities
   • Process days in order: Monday → Tuesday → Wednesday → Thursday → Friday → Saturday
   • For each day, sort time slots by instructor priority (1=highest preference)
   • Fill as many hours as possible on current day before moving to next
   • Respect max_continuous_hours constraint (e.g., TP courses need 2 continuous)
   • Result: Days are filled sequentially, honoring instructor preferences

STEP 2: Standard Gap-Filling (If needed)
   • Trigger: When Step 1 can't schedule all required hours
   • Fill remaining hours in ANY available slot
   • Still respects max_continuous_hours constraint
   • Result: Most gaps filled while maintaining quality

STEP 3: Ultra-Relaxed Gap-Filling (Final safety net)
   • Trigger: When Step 2 still leaves gaps
   • Schedule in ANY free slot - IGNORE max_continuous_hours
   • Only checks basic conflicts (instructor/room availability)
   • Result: Guarantees zero gaps - 100% completion

💡 INSTRUCTOR PRIORITY SYSTEM:

How Priorities Work:
   • Each instructor sets priorities (1-7) for each period on each day
   • Priority 1 = Highest preference (schedule here first)
   • Priority 7 = Lowest preference (use only if needed)
   • Unset priorities = 999 (avoid if possible)

Example:
   Instructor IT09 - Friday preferences:
      Period 4 (11:25-12:15): Priority 1 (most preferred)
      Period 7 (2:45-3:35):   Priority 2
      Period 3 (10:35-11:25): Priority 3
      Period 1 (8:45-9:45):   Priority 4
      ... and so on

   When scheduling on Friday, the algorithm will:
   1. Try Period 4 first (priority 1)
   2. Then Period 7 (priority 2)
   3. Then Period 3 (priority 3)
   4. Continue in priority order until all hours scheduled
